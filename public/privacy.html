Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This draft explains the intended handling of enquiries submitted through the Zumarad Studio website. It must be reviewed and adapted to the actual hosting, database, analytics and email providers before public launch.

Information collected

If you use the contact form, the service may collect your name, email address, company, project type, budget range, preferred timeline, message and consent to be contacted.

Purpose and retention

Information is used to review and respond to project enquiries, maintain appropriate business records and protect the service from abuse. Retention periods, deletion procedures and any legal obligations must be defined before production use.

Sharing and service providers

Enquiry data should not be sold. Hosting, database, email and security providers may process data as necessary to operate the service. No analytics or marketing trackers are intentionally included in this starter website.

Security

The application includes basic validation and rate limiting, but no website can promise absolute security. Production launch requires HTTPS, private database access, backups, access controls and operational checks.
